Cajun Potato Soup

Ingredients

  • 1 Cajun Sausage
  • 1 Clove Garlic
  • 1 Onion
  • 1 Red/Green Bell Pepper
  • 1 TBS Cajun Seasoning
  • 1/4 tsp Cayenne
  • 4 Russet Gold Potatos
  • 4 Cups Broth
  • 1 Cup Shredded Cheddar Cheese
  • 1/2 Cup Heavy Cream
  • Salt & Pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Cut the sausage into 1/2 inch pieces
  2. In a stock pot, brown the sausage on medium heat, flipping once
  3. While sausage is browning, dice garlic, onion and bell pepper
  4. Once browned on both sides, remove the sausage
  5. Add galic to the pot, browning slightly
  6. Add onion and cook until translucent, you can add a pinch of salt at this time
  7. Add bell pepper, cook until softened
  8. Wash and cut potatos into 1/2 inch cubes
  9. Add in potatos, broth, Cajun and Cayenne
  10. Simmer for 30 minutes or until potatos are fork tender
  11. Add in cheese and stir until melted
  12. Remove from heat and stir in cream
  13. Serve immedately
