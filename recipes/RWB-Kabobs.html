Red White and Blue Kabobs

Ingredients

  • Kabob Skewers
  • 1/2 lbs Strawberries
  • 1/4 lbs Blueberries
  • 1 angel food cake (optional marshmalows)

Steps

  1. Cube the angelfood cake into 1/2 inch pieces
  2. Wash your berries
  3. Cut your strawberries in half, remove the stem
  4. From the pointy end
  5. place 2 Blueberries on the skewer
  6. Place 1 Strawberry piece on the skewer
  7. Place 1 piece of angel food cake on the skewer
  8. Repeat until skewer is full
  9. Place in cake pan and refrigerate until needed
