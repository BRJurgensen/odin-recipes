Egg Hole Sandwiches

Ingredients

  • 1 Slice White Bread
  • 1 TBSP Butter
  • 1 Egg
  • Salt and Pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Heat frying pan to medium
  2. Take a jar and press into slice of bread, twisting to cut out the middle
  3. Add butter to frying pan
  4. Place bread in frying pan ensuring to not include the middle
  5. Crack egg and add in the middle of the bread
  6. Add salt and pepper to taste
  7. wait 3 minutes for medium runny yolk, adding or removing time according to preference
  8. Flip the sandwich
  9. Wait 1 minute, just long enough for the white of the egg to set
  10. Remove from pan, serve immediately with ketchup, hot sauce or hollindase
